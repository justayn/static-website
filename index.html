Every Christmas season since 2003, Maple Street and five of its neighbors deck out with more lights than is strictly reasonable — and everyone's welcome to walk through and see them, right up to Christmas Eve.

How our Christmas got out of hand

It started with three neighbors, a string of icicle lights, and too many extension cords. Nineteen Christmases later, it's forty houses, strings of tinsel on every porch, a shared spreadsheet for coordinating display nights, and a small but fierce rivalry over who gets the corner lot.

The tour is free, self-guided, and takes about forty minutes on foot at a reasonable pace — longer if you stop for cocoa and candy canes, which most people do.

  • First lit for Christmas 2003 by three houses on Maple Street
  • Now spans six blocks and roughly 40 participating homes
  • Santa Claus holds court at the corner of Maple & Holly on Saturdays, 6–8pm

The Christmas lights route, stop by stop

Start anywhere, but this is the order most people walk it in. About 0.6 miles end to end.

  1. 1

    412 Maple St. — where Christmas began here

    The original house. Still runs the same string-light arches over a lit-up Christmas tree, just more of them every year.

  2. 2

    Holly Avenue — the synchronized block

    Four houses in a row blink their lights to Christmas carols, broadcast on 89.1 FM as you walk past.

  3. 3

    Chestnut Court — the inflatable field

    A cul-de-sac given over entirely to oversized inflatable snowmen and reindeer. Bring kids, or don't; either way, look up.

  4. 4

    Corner of Maple & Holly — Santa's chair

    Weekend evenings only, through Christmas Eve. Bring your own camera; there's no official photographer this year.

  5. 5

    Birch Lane — the cocoa and candy cane stand

    Run by the block association, by donation. Usually open until the last house turns its Christmas lights off.

Before you head out

Parking

Street parking along Chestnut and the church lot on Birch Lane after 5pm. Please leave driveways clear for Christmas deliveries.

Getting around

The full loop is flat and stroller-friendly. Sidewalks narrow near Chestnut Court on busy Christmas nights.

What to bring

Warm layers, and a couple of dollars for the cocoa and candy cane stand if you'd like to support it.

Weather

The tour runs rain, snow, or shine, but lights are dimmed in heavy weather. Check tonight's forecast before you go.

Counting down to Christmas

Lights run December 1–24, dusk until 10pm, right up to Christmas Eve.
